# Amendment 002 — post-fit recovery and complete paired statistics

Authored after the nine training gates passed. No scientific method, data membership, model, seed, or training configuration changed.

The failed archive schema and repair are recorded in `FAILED_ATTEMPT_BOOTSTRAP_ARCHIVE.json`.

It passed. Historical immutability was rechecked (447/447 files) and all four detector cache bindings passed.
