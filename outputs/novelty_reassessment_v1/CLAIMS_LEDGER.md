# Claims ledger after novelty reassessment

This ledger replaces broad novelty language for planning purposes. It does not edit the historical manuscript or prior result packages. Primary evidence should be cited next to each statement in any future revision.

| Claim area | Supporting project evidence | Literature overlap | Permitted wording | Limits / wording to remove |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Training with reversal examples is a new method** | The project evaluates physics-aware training with three exact simulated limb-terminal swaps; in HEEDB RA–LL, mean TWA AUPRC was 0.5683 versus 0.1647 for clean-trained models, while clean AUPRC was 0.5760 versus 0.5726. Chapman/Ningbo ResNet and Chapman Transformer packages contain related results. | Cao et al. (2020) already proposed a reversal-robust architecture and tested it on RA/LL, cycles and other limb swaps without reporting reversal training. 3KG and later physically motivated augmentation works also predate this project, although their transformations are not the same cable-fault intervention. | “We evaluate a physically derived reversal-training intervention for three project endpoints and compare its behavior with existing baselines in the tested cohorts.” | Remove “first reversal-robust ECG model,” “first training on physically valid reversals,” and any claim that the augmentation operation itself is novel. Do not imply Cao used augmentation. |
| **B. Missing-lead tests do not fully characterize physical reversals** | Corrected Ningbo mask recovery: for baseline ResNet, mean five-lead-removal AUPRC exceeded RA–LL by 0.4042 (synchronized record bootstrap 95% CI 0.3809–0.4257); exact five-lead mask exceeded RA–LL by 0.2536 (0.2316–0.2778). In physics-aware M2, mean mask-minus-RA–LL was −0.0616 (−0.0707 to −0.0526), exact-mask contrast −0.0405. The chest-only six-run package found unchanged scores under the three limb swaps by construction/inference audit, and ordinary 12-lead RA–LL AUPRC fell by about 0.325 on Chapman and 0.494 on Ningbo versus chest-only. | Cao already demonstrates classifier testing under coherent lead swaps; lead masking is separately studied by Oh et al. and Bo et al. Current project adds a specific comparison design rather than the general observation that reversal can matter. | “In the corrected Ningbo ResNet analysis, a predeclared exact-mask/mask-panel contrast distinguished the tested RA–LL permutation from the tested five-lead removals; the magnitude and direction depended on training arm.” | Do not say “missingness testing is invalid,” “masks always underestimate failure,” or that masks and cable faults are physically equivalent. Results are model-, endpoint-, source- and mask-definition-specific; corrected Ningbo is a reanalysis after prior test access. Do not reuse the pre-recovery Ningbo mask values from the manuscript draft. |
| **C. Protection strategies have a single winner** | Chest-only results: physics-aware minus chest-only RA–LL TWA AUPRC was Chapman −0.0131 (95% CI −0.0282 to +0.0008) and Ningbo +0.0111 (−0.0014 to +0.0231); both intervals include zero. Huang→predicted correction→clean ResNet reached HEEDB RA–LL AUPRC 0.5811 versus 0.5683 physics-aware; physics-aware minus Huang was −0.0128 (−0.0280 to +0.0019), while AUROC, specificity, sensitivity and calibration tradeoffs differed. | Huang et al. (2024) publicly release a detector and models; Li et al. show a wearable detect/filter workflow; Cao et al. show architectural robustness. | “Across the evaluated settings, augmentation improved a vulnerable 12-lead model under simulated RA–LL, while chest-only and detection/correction were competitive alternatives; primary-endpoint superiority over chest-only or Huang was not established.” | Remove “augmentation is best,” “augmentation beats using chest leads,” “beats Huang,” “equivalent to Huang,” and “calibration is preserved.” The Huang comparison was selected after HEEDB outcomes were seen and is exploratory. |
| **D. Robustness transfers across architectures and populations** | ResNet outcomes exist across Chapman, Ningbo and a patient-disjoint HEEDB subgroup; HEEDB physician-code definitions are proxy endpoints from the same Emory repository. The corrected Transformer Chapman Stage M3A gate **failed** on one seed's mask retention (−0.1126), and Transformer Ningbo was not run. Founder was frozen-backbone with clean-trained head; no mitigation was tested. | Cao's architecture result, Huang's detector external tests, wearable workflow, and Lee et al. 2025 LVSD reversal stress test all narrow broad transfer claims. | “The ResNet finding recurred across these tested datasets and the specified HEEDB proxy cohort; this is evidence of transport across the evaluated sources, not general architecture invariance.” | Remove “architecture independent,” “population independent,” “externally validated in multiple independent centers,” or any use of M3 as a successful replication. HEEDB confirmation is same repository/source family; tests have historical access; labels are proxy. |
| **E. The method is computationally more efficient** | Existing chest-only report includes training duration (~1 h 43 min total for six fits) and measured GPU memory, but there is no fully matched, controlled compute-cost comparison against Cao, Huang, masking, or chest-only inference across identical hardware/workloads. | Huang is explicitly lightweight and publicly deployable; their article targets efficient detection. | No efficiency contribution is currently established. Report hardware/runtime only as descriptive implementation details if needed. | Remove “lower cost,” “more efficient,” and “deployment-ready” absent a matched workload and latency/memory/power study. |
| **Physical realism and clinical meaning** | The project implements algebraically exact simulated terminal swaps; all tested project faults remain simulated. The HEEDB 12SL label sets remain proxy constructs; the manuscript requests clinician review. | Krasteva et al. (2019) supports deriving terminal swaps including WCT changes; real clinical/reversal detection studies exist, but the reviewed project has no adjudicated clinical cable faults. | “Simulated terminal-electrode permutations under the project's stated electrical model.” | Do not write “real miswiring,” “patient benefit,” “more patients detected,” or “clinically validated.” AUPRC gain does not establish additional case detection. |
