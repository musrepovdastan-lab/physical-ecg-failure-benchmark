# Claims and evidence — current synthesis

Version 3, 1 November 2026. AP refers to TWA raw average precision unless specified. Point estimates are full-cohort means of three seed-specific metrics; intervals are unadjusted conditional paired-bootstrap intervals, not between-seed confidence intervals.

| Claim | Evidence and allowed wording | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Initialization is controlled in the corrected role-control series. | Initial tensors/RNG were saved; 18 fresh-process checks verified same-seed identity across methods and different-seed differences. Nine fits passed the prespecified gates. | This does not independently repeat all nine full trajectories. Original starting weights are unknown. Explicit RNG-stream isolation/determinism also changed; old/new differences do not isolate an initialization-only causal effect. |
| Reversal-aware training still improves AP over ordinary in the tested intermediate mixtures. | Equal-three q=0.1 p050−ordinary: Ningbo +0.1378 [0.1243, 0.1503], HEEDB +0.1018 [0.0905, 0.1130]. Combined: +0.1187 [0.1066, 0.1313] and +0.0951 [0.0839, 0.1058]. [Table 2](SEED_CORRECTED_RESULTS.md). | Specified simulated mixtures, models and coded/proxy endpoints; not all architectures, faults or deployments. q is not observed prevalence. |
| The point leader changes with fit series/cohort/q. | Ningbo clean/intermediate-q leader changed from combined to p050; HEEDB retained p050. Huang retained point leadership at q=1. [Table 4](SEED_CORRECTED_RESULTS.md). | Point leadership does not establish pairwise superiority. No universal winner or optimized augmentation probability. |
| AP superiority over Huang at equal-three q=0.1 is not supported by the corrected intervals. | At equal-three q=0.1 all four p050/combined-minus-Huang cohort comparisons include zero. In particular p050−Huang is +0.0126 [−0.0002, 0.0248] on Ningbo and +0.0065 [−0.0056, 0.0174] on HEEDB. [Table 3](SEED_CORRECTED_RESULTS.md). | Historical role-control positive comparisons must not be presented as results of the corrected series. Intervals including zero establish neither equivalence nor an absence of an effect. |
| Huang's high-fault point advantage has a qualified interpretation. | At equal-three q=1, p050−Huang is −0.0060 [−0.0227, 0.0088] on Ningbo and −0.0218 [−0.0362, −0.0100] on HEEDB; Huang also exceeds combined by intervals in both cohorts. | Ningbo advantage over p050 is a point ranking only. Huang uses a frozen detector plus new ordinary classifiers, not a new detector training or reproduction of the original paper. |
| Probability and operating-point tradeoffs remain distinct from AP. | At equal-three q=0.1, both augmentation arms improve calibrated Brier and reduce false positives against ordinary in both cohorts. Combined's Ningbo sensitivity interval includes zero. On clean HEEDB p050/combined add approximately 10.9/7.6 false positives per 1,000 evaluated ECGs. | Source-clean q90 thresholds are fixed. False positives per 1,000 ECGs are not false positives per 1,000 negatives. Better Brier does not establish clinical calibration or utility. |
| Historical mask and reversal comparisons remain useful controls. | [Corrected mask report](../mask_semantics_recovery_v2/FINAL_RECOVERY_REPORT.md): removing five affected displayed leads and physically reversing terminals are distinct interventions. | These historical models are not the corrected nine-fit series. The 792-mask panel was not rerun on the new models. Earlier inverted-mask outputs remain superseded. |
| Chest-only, architecture and cycle results bound transfer claims. | Historical chest-only outputs are invariant to limb swaps/cycles. M3 failed its complete retention gate. Two cycle compositions were evaluated on historical models; identity re-derivation did not materially alter those cycle contrasts. [Sources](SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md). | No re-evaluation of all these controls with corrected checkpoints. No real miswirings, universal architecture transfer or equivalence claim. |
| The contribution is a bounded comparative evaluation. | A specified physical fault family, channel-removal controls, training recipes and detector/correction pipeline expose different tradeoffs. Prior literature already studies reversal robustness. | No first-method, SOTA, broad novelty, causal masking effect, clinical benefit or superiority over Cao claim. |

All HEEDB outcomes remain coded proxy targets and the cohort was previously examined. Ningbo separation is established at record/waveform level, not patient level. Both datasets are reused for the correction; no reserved cohort or new external center was evaluated. The current manuscript combines separately bound experiment families and labels them explicitly rather than treating them as a single common-model leaderboard.
