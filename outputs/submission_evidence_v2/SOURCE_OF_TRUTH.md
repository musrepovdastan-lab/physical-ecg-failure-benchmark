## Exact signal conventions

Canonical channel order is `I, II, III, aVR, aVL, aVF, V1, V2, V3, V4, V5, V6`. The exact RA–LL mask removes indices `[0,1,2,3,5]` (`I, II, III, aVR, aVF`) and leaves `aVL, V1–V6`. A physical RA–LL terminal reversal is a separate algebraic transform: the derived leads `I, II, III, aVR, aVF` change, while `aVL` and `V1–V6` remain invariant. A missing-lead mask is not waveform-equivalent to that reversal.

## Interpretation rule

Use endpoint-, dataset-, model-, and condition-specific language. Bootstrap intervals condition on selected checkpoints/calibrators; three training seeds provide limited training-variability coverage. The external label constructs remain proxies; real acquired miswirings, held-out reversal/composition generalization, prospective workflow utility, and clinician-approved endpoint mappings remain untested.
