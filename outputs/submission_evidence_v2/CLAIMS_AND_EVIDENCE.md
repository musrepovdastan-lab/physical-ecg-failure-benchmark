## Cross-cutting limits

- The revised package is a synthesis of existing experiments, corrected reanalyses and exploratory extensions; it is not a new experiment.
- Two patient-disjoint HEEDB cohorts are from Emory. They are external to Chapman/Ningbo development sources but are not two external centers.
- HEEDB endpoints use physician code sets as proxy labels, not adjudicated disease diagnoses or exact SNOMED replication. Clinician confirmation is pending.
- No real registered lead miswirings, held-out reversal compositions, or reserved-test evaluation were performed in the six-method extension.
- Intervals in multi-method HEEDB comparisons are conditional on selected checkpoints, calibrators and thresholds. Three seeds incompletely capture training uncertainty; no universal/equivalence inference follows.
- q_fault and p_aug denote different quantities. q_fault is an evaluation mixture scenario; p_aug is the training-example allocation. Neither is an empirical prevalence estimate.
- No manuscript claim should refer to patient identifiers, individual predictions, or restricted waveforms.
