# MIXED_SIGNAL_REQUIRES_NEW_CONFIRMATORY_PROTOCOL

The v4 data show substantial variation across nominal masks, but V1–V4 was less damaging than the average comparator mask, not more damaging. Because nominal independent masks include prediction-equivalent availability abstractions and the v4 gate used an absolute signed contrast, the result cannot confirm the proposed mechanism or justify multiseed work as frozen. A new, directionally specified, severity-matched confirmatory protocol is required.
